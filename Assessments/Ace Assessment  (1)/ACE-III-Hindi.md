## Overview & Provenance

This document contains the complete clinical text, instructions, questions, word lists, and stimuli for the **ACE-III Hindi Edition** as implemented in the ACE-III HTML assessment. It is typeset in **Noto Sans Devanagari** script, uses Devanagari numerals, and incorporates Indian cultural adaptations (Father of the Nation question, Mall Road Shimla address, Devanagari fragmented letters).

### Task 2: Word registration (`att-registration`)
- **Domain:** ATTENTION | **Max Score:** 3

#### Spoken Administration Script:
- **[str-registration-script]**
  - **Target Script:** मैं तीन शब्द बोलूँगा/बोलूँगी, मेरे बोलने के बाद दोहराइए।
  - **English Reference:** *I'm going to say three words. Please repeat them after me.*

#### Instructions & Guidance Notes:
- **[str-registration-sub]**
  - **Target Script:** शब्द एक-एक करके बोलिए: {w1}... {w2}... {w3} — हर शब्द के बीच कम से कम २ सेकंड का विराम रखिए। फिर कहिए: «शब्द याद रखिए, मैं बाद में दुबारा आपसे पूछूँगा/पूछूँगी।»
  - **English Reference:** *Say the words one by one with a pause in between: नींबू- Nimbu, चाबी- Chaabi, गेंद- Gend. Give a pause of at least 2 seconds between the words. Then say: "Try to remember them because I'm going to ask you later." You have the ability to choose the trial for scoring.*
- **[str-registration-remember]**
  - **Target Script:** शब्द याद रखिए, मैं बाद में दुबारा आपसे पूछूँगा/पूछूँगी।
  - **English Reference:** *Try to remember them because I'm going to ask you later.*

### Task 3: Serial 7s (`att-serial7s`)
- **Domain:** ATTENTION | **Max Score:** 5

#### Spoken Administration Script:
- **[str-serial7s-script]**
  - **Target Script:** सौ (१००) में से सात (७) घटाते जाइए, जब तक मैं न रोकूँ।
  - **English Reference:** *Could you take 7 away from 100? Then keep taking 7 away from each number you give me, until I tell you to stop.*

#### Instructions & Guidance Notes:
- **[str-serial7s-sub]**
  - **Target Script:** गलत जवाब देने पर उन्हें न रोकें। पाँच उत्तरों के बाद परीक्षण रोक दें और अंत में उनके जवाबों का आंकलन करें।
  - **English Reference:** *Do not correct or stop them. Stop after 5 answers. Write all responses clearly in the boxes provided.*

#### Serial 7s Alternative Administration:
- **Spell Backward Script:** प्रतिभागी से कहें कि वे “धर्मशाला” शब्द को उल्टा बोलें/लिखें और उत्तर लिखें। *(English: Ask the participant to spell the word “Dharmshala” backwards and record the answer.)*
- **Spell Backward Guidance:** उत्तर लिखें और यदि प्रतिभागी गलती करे तो उन्हें न रोकें। उन्हें पूरा करने तक जारी रखने दें। *(English: Record responses and do not stop the participant if they make a mistake. Allow them to continue until they finish.)*
- **Months Backward Script:** प्रतिभागी से कहें कि वे दिसंबर से शुरू करते हुए महीनों के नाम उल्टे क्रम में बोलें और उत्तर लिखें। प्रतिभागी से कहें कि वे महीनों के नाम उल्टे क्रम में बोलते रहें जब तक आप उन्हें रुकने के लिए न कहें। 5 महीने पूरे होने के बाद प्रतिभागी को रोक दें। *(English: Ask the participant to say the months in reverse order starting from December and record the answers. Ask the participant to keep reversing the months until you ask them to stop. Stop the participant after 5 months.)*

### Task 5: Letter fluency (`flu-letter`)
- **Domain:** FLUENCY | **Max Score:** 7

#### Spoken Administration Script:
- **[str-fluency-letter-script]**
  - **Target Script:** मैं आपको एक अक्षर दूँगा/दूँगी, उस अक्षर से शुरू होने वाले जितने शब्द आप बता सकते हैं बताइये। इनमें इंसान के और जगह के नाम नहीं होने चाहिए।

#### Instructions & Guidance Notes:
- **[str-fluency-letter-sub1]**

- **[str-fluency-letter-sub2]**
  - **Target Script:** अभी आप "प" से शुरू होने वाले शब्द बताइए, सभी मात्राओ का प्रयोग कर सकते हैं, जैसे (पि, पु, पे, पै) आपको एक मिनट का समय दिया जाएगा।

### Task 10: Sentence writing (`lang-writing`)
- **Domain:** LANGUAGE | **Max Score:** 2

#### Spoken Administration Script:
- **[str-writing-script]**
  - **Target Script:** अपनी पिछली छुट्टी (शनिवार/इतवार या त्यौहार) के बारे में दो या दो से अधिक वाक्य लिखिए।
  - **English Reference:** *I'd like you to write two sentences. They can be about anything you like. Please write in full sentences. No abbreviations.*
- **[str-writing-pref-script]**
  - **Target Script:** कृपया अपनी पहली भाषा में, या किसी भी पसंदीदा भाषा में, दो पूरे वाक्य लिखिए। विषय आपकी पसंद का हो सकता है।
  - **English Reference:** *I'd like you to write two sentences in your first language, or any language you prefer. They can be about anything you like. Please write in full sentences. No abbreviations.*

#### Instructions & Guidance Notes:
- **[str-writing-sub]**
  - **Target Script:** अगर दोनों वाक्य एक ही विषय पर लिखे गए हों तो १ अंक दें, और अगर व्याकरण तथा शब्द सही हों तो १ अंक दें।
  - **English Reference:** *If they're unsure what to write, suggest a few options: 'You could write about a recent holiday, your hobbies, your family, or your childhood.' If the patient writes only one sentence, gently ask for a second one.*
- **[str-writing-topics]**
  - **Target Script:** सुझाए गए विषय: पिछली छुट्टी / शनिवार-इतवार / त्यौहार।
  - **English Reference:** *Suggest topics: recent holiday / hobbies / family / childhood.*
- **[str-writing-pref-sub]**
  - **Target Script:** अगर वे तय न कर पाएँ कि क्या लिखें तो विषय सुझाइए (जैसे पिछली छुट्टी, शौक, परिवार या बचपन)। केवल एक वाक्य लिखा जाए तो दूसरे वाक्य के लिए धीरे से कहिए।
  - **English Reference:** *If they're unsure what to write, suggest topics (e.g. a recent holiday, hobbies, family or childhood). If only one sentence is written, gently ask for a second.*

#### Suggested Sentence Topics:
- पिछली छुट्टी
- शनिवार-इतवार
- त्यौहार

## Clinical Administration & Scoring Guidelines

These guidelines specify exact administration rules and clinical scoring criteria.

### Administration & Scoring Rules:
- **`instr-repeat-1`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Score only the first attempt.*
- **`instr-repeat-2`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Do not accept responses that are halting, slurred, or clearly mispronounced.*
- **`instr-repeat-scoring`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *2 points – All 4 words repeated correctly. 1 point – 3 words correct. 0 points – 2 or fewer correct.*
- **`instr-proverbs-1`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Wait for the patient to respond before saying the next.*
- **`instr-proverbs-scoring`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *1 point per proverb if repeated exactly and clearly. 0 points for partial or altered repetition (e.g. wrong words or word order) or slurred, hesitant, or incomplete responses.*
- **`instr-naming-1`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *No clues, hints, or gestures.*
- **`instr-naming-2`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Accept common accurate names (e.g. crocodile/alligator).*
- **`instr-naming-scoring`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *1 point per correct answer (max 12). No partial credit.*
- **`instr-pointing-1`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *No hints or rephrasing.*
- **`instr-pointing-2`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Repeat the question once if asked.*
- **`instr-pointing-scoring`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *1 point for each correct picture (max 4).*
- **`instr-reading-1`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *No help or correction allowed.*
- **`instr-reading-scoring`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Score 1 only if all 5 words are read correctly. 0 otherwise.*
- **`instr-dots-1`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Ask patient to count dots in each box without pointing.*
- **`instr-dots-scoring`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Give 1 point for each correct answer (max 4 points).*
- **`instr-letters-1`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Ask patient to identify the letter in each box.*
- **`instr-letters-scoring`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Give 1 point for each correct answer (max 4 points).*
- **`instr-writing-1`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Patient must write two complete sentences.*
- **`instr-writing-2`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *No abbreviations allowed.*
- **`instr-writing-3`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Suggest topics if patient is unsure.*
- **`instr-writing-4`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Each sentence is assessed separately.*
- **`instr-writing-scoring`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *One point per sentence that has both a subject and a verb. Minor grammar errors do not affect the score.*
- **`instr-jumbled-scoring`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Both sentences in correct order — score 2. One sentence in correct order — score 1. Both sentences wrong — score 0.*
- **`instr-address-learning-scoring`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Score only the third trial. Score 1 point for each correct element recalled (max 7 points).*
- **`instr-recall-address-1`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Allow the patient to recall freely without prompting.*
- **`instr-recall-address-2`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *After free recall, use recognition prompts if needed (max 5 additional points).*
- **`instr-recall-address-scoring`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Give 1 point for each correctly recalled element (max 7 points). Total possible score across recall and recognition: 12 points (7 recall + 5 recognition).*
- **`instr-words-recall`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Mark each word as correct or incorrect based on patient's response.*

### Clinical Notes & Edge Cases:
- **`guid-orient-one-at-a-time`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Ask items one at a time.*
- **`guid-orient-month-name`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *If the month is given as a number, prompt for the month name.*
- **`guid-season-transition`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Season change: if the answer is autumn or summer, accept after the follow-up 'Could it be another season?' — do not accept winter or spring.*
- **`guid-orient-place-substitution`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *At home use the building or complex name; substitute room for floor; in a single-storey setting use a local landmark; score out-of-county patients somewhat more liberally.*
- **`guid-orient-place-written`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Aphasic patients may write their answers.*
- **`guid-registration-trials`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Repeat trials 2–3 only if needed; the number of trials is recorded but not scored.*
- **`guid-serial7s`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Do not correct or stop the patient; stop after five subtractions.*
- **`guid-recall-words-no-prompt`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Do not prompt.*
- **`guid-fluency-letter-exclusions`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Not counted: repetitions; perseverations (pay/paid/pays = 1); intrusions; proper names; plurals (pot/pots = 1).*
- **`guid-fluency-animals-exclusions`**:
  - **English Guideline:** *Superordinate not counted when exemplars given (fish + salmon + trout = 2); no sex/age variants (deer/doe/fawn/stag = 1); all animal types accepted, including mythical; if stuck on the previous letter (panda, possum…), remind the patient any letter is allowed.*
- **`guid-address-wait`**:
